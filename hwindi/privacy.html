Hwindi

Privacy policy

In force from 13 November 2026

Hwindi is a game for iPhone and Android phones, made and published by Takunda Mugaradziko of Harare, Zimbabwe ("I", "me"). This page says what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game itself collects nothing about you, and the adverts in it are supplied by Google, which does collect some.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress (fares, vehicles, best distance, settings and how you have played) is kept on your phone only. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of mine that your phone talks to. I never receive your name, your email address, your contacts, your photos or your location, because the game never asks for them.

On Android, your phone's own backup may include the saved game if you have backup switched on. That copy is yours and is handled by Google under your Google account.

Adverts

The game shows adverts through Google AdMob. To choose, show and count adverts, and to stop fraud, Google's advert software inside the game collects information from your phone and sends it to Google. This can include:

  • your phone's advertising identifier (on Android), and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which a rough location such as your city or country can be worked out;
  • the make and model of your phone, its system version, language and network;
  • which adverts you were shown and whether you watched or tapped them;
  • technical reports about how the advert software ran.

Google handles this information under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. I can see totals in my AdMob account, such as how many adverts were shown and what they earned. I cannot see who you are.

Your choices about adverts

  • iPhone. The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it is not given your phone's advertising identifier.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or turn off personalised adverts, in Settings > Google > Ads (on some phones: Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Europe and the United Kingdom. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your choice about adverts before any are requested, and you can change that choice later.
  • You can also manage how Google personalises adverts for you at adssettings.google.com.

What the game is allowed to do on your phone

  • Internet. Used only to fetch adverts. The game itself is complete on your phone and plays offline.
  • Network state. So the game knows whether an advert could reach the phone.
  • Vibration. For the bumps and crashes. You can switch it off in the game.

Children

Hwindi is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given information through the game, write to me and I will help.

Removing your information

Deleting the game removes everything it kept on your phone. For information held by Google, use the Google links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
