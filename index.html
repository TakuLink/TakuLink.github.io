Hwindi

Town! Town! Town! A kombi runner set on Zimbabwe's roads.

You drive the kombi and the hwindi hangs out of the door. Load passengers, dodge the traffic, keep the tank from running dry, and get as many metres down the road as you can before the police, a pothole or an empty tank ends the trip.

Help

Something not working, or an idea for the game?
Write to takumugaradziko@gmail.com. Say which phone you have and what happened, and you will get an answer.

Common questions

Does the game need the internet?
No. The whole game is on your phone and plays offline. A connection is only used for adverts.

Where is my progress kept?
On your phone. There is no account and nothing to sign in to. If you delete the game, your progress goes with it.

Why do I see adverts?
The game is free. Adverts pay for it. Most are ones you choose to watch, for a second chance after a crash or to double a trip's fares.

How do I turn the sound or the vibration off?
Use the buttons at the top of the rank screen.
